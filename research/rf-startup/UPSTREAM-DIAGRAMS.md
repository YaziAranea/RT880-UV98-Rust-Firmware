# Manufacturer schematic references

The historical RF research used a user-supplied `RT880 V2.pdf`, pages 1–3.
Its recorded SHA256 is:

```text
b7858c30fbe6eb8bf57967d57d9985b29951ccffa766ca5ac9240796c9fab32a
```

No canonical upstream URL or redistribution license was recorded for that PDF.
The page renders and crops below are therefore excluded from the public source
tree, sanitized Git history and release archives. They are manufacturer schematic
excerpts, not photographs of the owner's UV98 and not original project artwork.

The original bytes were preserved and verified in the owner's private archive
before removal. The hashes below identify those research inputs without
redistributing them. Historical notes still mention their old filenames;
firmware/CPS builds and tests do not require these PNG files. Pin observations,
analysis evidence and the limitations recorded in [EVIDENCE-RU.txt](EVIDENCE-RU.txt)
remain available. Those observations do not establish another PCB's compatibility.

All filenames below were relative to `research/rf-startup/`.
